# Public Data Manifest

## Included Materials

This repository contains the materials needed to inspect and reproduce the manuscript's stated evidence boundary:

- source code, environment definitions, parameter registry, and numbered entry points;
- the anonymous 20-node CAD-derived topology and disclosed synthetic task-flow assumptions;
- frozen checkpoints, training metadata, and transition caches used by the principal staged models;
- authoritative confirmatory outputs for the physics factorial, action-ranking, shadow, formulation-boundary, and nongraph-boundary analyses;
- processed AnyLogic and Python validation exports;
- publication figures, editable vector files, and figure source data;
- automated tests, provenance records, and claim-to-evidence mappings.

The stochastic task streams are generated by the disclosed simulator. They are not factory production records.

## Intentionally Excluded Materials

The following materials are not part of the public release:

- the restricted source CAD drawing and all company-identifying annotations;
- company names, product identifiers, architectural details, and production records;
- manuscript drafts, correspondence, reviewer material, and local workstation files;
- superseded exploratory runs that do not support a manuscript claim;
- large per-decision traces from development-only boundary runs, because they are regenerable and their episode summaries and audit reports are retained;
- redundant scenario renders and journal-upload TIFF copies that can be rebuilt from the included source data.

## Large Retained Files

The paired physical and position prediction tables are retained because `analyze_v11_physics_factorial.py` reads them directly to reproduce the principal multistep factorial statistics. Shared transition caches are retained to make the staged training protocol reproducible without recollecting trajectories.

## Confidentiality Boundary

The public topology is a geometrically simplified and de-identified derivative. It preserves decision-relevant connectivity and metric edge lengths but cannot be used to reconstruct the restricted factory CAD. The AnyLogic project uses the same anonymous topology and validates capacity and congestion trends only.

## Versioning

The manuscript should cite an immutable archived release rather than the moving `main` branch. Reuse terms are defined in `LICENSE_SCOPE.md`; the archival identifier will be added after the frozen release is deposited.
